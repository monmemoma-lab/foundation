A commitment to human dignity

Who we are

A purpose rooted in family legacy, grounded in community.

Established in 2022, the Dabir–Sohani–Suraiya Foundation is a registered, voluntary, non-profit organisation with deep roots in Rupganj, Narayanganj. The foundation carries forward a tradition of responsibility to the communities connected with the wider Salma Group family.

Its mission is straightforward: to create meaningful opportunities for people whose ambitions or wellbeing may be constrained by financial circumstances. The foundation begins with education because learning can change the direction of an entire life. It also recognises that lasting progress depends on the health, stability and dignity of families.

Rather than treating philanthropy as a single event, the foundation seeks continuity through established partnerships, practical assistance and a growing understanding of community needs. Its documented work includes university scholarships and learning materials for children, alongside wider welfare commitments.

Our flagship education programme

One scholarship can change the course of a future.

30 scholarships awarded to date.

In December 2023, the foundation established a formal scholarship partnership with the Faculty of Business Studies at the University of Dhaka. The inaugural programme provided scholarships to ten students, supporting academic merit alongside financial need.

The relationship has continued through subsequent scholarship ceremonies in May 2025 and April 2026. These events brought together students, university representatives and foundation leadership to recognise determination and encourage continued study.

Across the scholarship programme, the foundation has now awarded 30 scholarships. The cumulative total speaks to a continuing commitment: helping young people complete their education and build the skills needed to contribute to their communities.

Who it supportsAcademically promising students experiencing financial constraints
Institutional partnerFaculty of Business Studies, University of Dhaka

From the first school day forward

Every child deserves to begin with confidence.

Education support starts long before university. For families balancing daily responsibilities, basic supplies such as school bags and learning materials can make an important difference to a child's experience of school.

On 23 January 2026, the foundation organised an education-materials distribution programme for children from families connected with Salma Group's factories. Approximately 850 schoolchildren received school bags and learning items, with the event bringing together children, their parents, foundation members and guests.

This initiative complements the university scholarship programme. Together, they reflect support at different stages of the educational journey — encouraging children at the outset and helping students pursue higher learning as they grow.

Announced initiative

Caring for a mother. Welcoming a new life.

A healthy beginning matters to both mother and child. Recognising the needs of women working in Salma Group factories, the foundation has announced a dedicated maternity-welfare initiative for expecting mothers employed at Salma Group factories.

Under this initiative, each expecting worker mother will receive a nutrition package valued at ৳5,000, intended to support maternal nourishment, together with a ৳5,000 prize bond for her child. The programme combines practical care in the present with a gift that symbolises hope for the child's future.

The initiative reflects a broader belief that supporting working families helps communities grow stronger. It sits alongside the foundation's wider interest in health-related hardship and family welfare.

Our wider commitment

Helping communities thrive in ways that last.

The foundation's documented education initiatives are part of a broader vision of community wellbeing. These areas describe continuing priorities and potential directions for development.

♡

Healthcare & support in hardship

Considering assistance for people struggling with essential medical needs and periods of family difficulty. Protecting individual privacy and approaching requests with dignity are important to this work.

✿

Learning spaces & community institutions

Supporting access to books, libraries, educational resources and community institutions that bring people together. These priorities complement existing scholarship and school-supply work.

♧

Environment & future generations

Encouraging environmental stewardship, greenery and healthier surroundings as part of a long-term ambition to strengthen the places where children and families live.

These are areas of the foundation's wider social commitment; activities and assistance remain subject to review and available resources.
